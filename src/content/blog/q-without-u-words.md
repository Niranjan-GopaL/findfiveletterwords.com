The Q is worth ten points and most of the time it isn't worth the trouble: it sits on your rack, waiting for a U that never comes, until you finally dump it for nothing. The fix is a short, learnable list — the **Q-without-U words** — and it changes how the tile feels forever.

## The essential core

Start with the words you'll actually play, ranked roughly by usefulness:

- **QI** — life force in Chinese philosophy. The single most important Q-word in the game: two tiles, playable almost anywhere, monstrous on a triple-letter square.
- **QAT** — a chewable shrub (variant of khat). Three letters, clean hook potential.
- **QIS / QATS** — the plurals, which is how QI becomes a 60-point parallel play.
- **QOPH** — the nineteenth letter of the Hebrew alphabet, also spelled KOPH.
- **QANAT** — a Persian irrigation tunnel; the classic five-letter Q-without-U play. It lives with its relatives on our [Q-but-no-U list](/5-letter-words/with-q-but-no-u/).
- **TRANQ** — the rare *Q-final* word, which makes it a hook target on the board's right edge.

## The deeper bench

Once the core feels natural, the longer entries start appearing in your games: FAQIR (a variant of fakir), QINDAR and QINTAR (Albanian currency), SHEQEL (the Israeli currency, plural SHEQALIM), and NIQAB. International CSW play adds more still — the British list has always been friendlier to transliterated vocabulary.

Notice the pattern: nearly every Q-without-U word is a **borrowing** — from Mandarin, Arabic, Hebrew, Persian, Albanian. English itself basically refuses to spell /k/ with a bare Q, so the exceptions arrive by import. That's also why the list is short enough to memorize: the lending languages are doing all the work.

## Rack strategy with the Q

Knowing the words is half the skill; the other half is timing.

1. **Don't hoard.** If QI is playable for 20+ points, play it. Waiting for the dream QUIXOTIC setup costs more in stuck turns than it ever pays out.
2. **Count the U's.** Four U's exist in a standard set. If three are on the board and you don't hold the fourth, your Q is a QI/QAT mission, full stop.
3. **Late game, dump at any price.** An unplayed Q is *minus* ten points at the buzzer. A four-point QI in the corner beats elegance.

## Does this help in Wordle too?

Occasionally — and memorably. Wordle has dabbled in Q-words that bend the rules, and when the yellow Q appears with no U confirmed, most players freeze. You won't: you'll already be thinking of the [five-letter Q lists](/5-letter-words/with-q/) instead of staring at the keyboard.

Ten minutes of memorization, one tile permanently defused.
